Obstacle Motor Wiring

Arduino Uno, HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, 16x2 I2C LCD, one BO motor driven through an L298N from a 9V battery, and a status LED. Every pin matches arduino_obstacle_motor.ino.

Before you power it on

  • One motor only. It uses channel A of the L298N (ENA, IN1, IN2, OUT1, OUT2). Leave ENB, IN3, IN4, OUT3 and OUT4 empty.
  • Remove the ENA jumper cap so D5 can set the speed. With the cap on, the motor always runs at full speed.
  • Join the grounds. All GND tags in the diagram are one connection: Arduino GND, sensor, LCD, LED, L298N GND and the battery −. Without it the motor won't respond.
  • Leave the L298N 5V terminal unconnected. The Arduino gets its power from USB. The 9V battery powers only the motor.
  • Wheel turning backwards? Swap the two motor wires on OUT1 and OUT2.
